Should Board Exams Be Abolished?

Board exams decide a lot about a student's future in India. I believe they should be abolished and replaced with continuous assessment through the year.

The main problem with board exams is pressure. Students study for months for a few days of exams, and one bad day can affect college admissions. Every year we hear about students who are extremely stressed during the exam season. Continuous assessment would spread the pressure across the year so no single day decides everything.

Board exams also test memory more than understanding. Many students memorise answers from guide books without understanding the topic, because that is what scores marks. Projects, presentations and class tests would show what a student can actually do.

Some people say board exams are fair because everyone writes the same paper under the same conditions, while school marks can be inflated. I agree this is a risk, but it could be reduced by having outside moderators check a sample of school marks.

Board exams have been used for a long time but that does not mean they are the best system. Continuous assessment is fairer and less stressful.
